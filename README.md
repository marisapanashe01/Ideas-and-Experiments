The goal is simple: explore possibilities, test assumptions, and learn by making things.

## 🧭 How I Explore an Idea

1. **💭 Question** — Identify a problem, curiosity, or opportunity.
2. **🔬 Experiment** — Build a small prototype, test a concept, or investigate a technical approach.
3. **📊 Evaluate** — Observe the results, limitations, and what could be improved.
4. **📝 Document** — Record what worked, what didn't, and what I learned.
5. **🚀 Develop further** — Decide whether the idea deserves a more complete implementation.

---

## 🧰 A Playground for Learning

Experiments may involve C#, .NET, Linux, cybersecurity tools, AI-assisted development, or technologies I haven't worked with before.

Not every experiment will become a finished application. Some will remain small demonstrations; others may evolve into full projects with their own repositories.

Where security is involved, experiments will use authorised environments and appropriate test data.

---

## 🌱 What's the Point?

To stay curious, test ideas instead of merely imagining them, and develop the confidence to work through unfamiliar technical problems.

**An idea is a starting point. Experimentation is how it becomes understanding.**
